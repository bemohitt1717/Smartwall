# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

SmartWall serves homeowners, renters, and design-minded buyers who want to imagine paint colors in their own rooms before making a purchase. They are usually browsing on a phone or laptop in a casual planning moment, looking for confidence and clarity rather than a complex design workflow.

## Product Purpose

SmartWall helps visitors explore paint color possibilities through an intuitive visualizer experience that makes room transformation feel immediate and low-pressure. Success looks like strong interest in the product, clear understanding of the value proposition, and a smooth path from first impression to trying the color experience.

## Positioning

SmartWall turns room repainting from a risky guess into a quick, confidence-building visual decision.

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel modern, confident, and design-forward without becoming overly technical or cold. It should communicate ease, inspiration, and premium clarity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS-style visual clutter, overly dark developer aesthetics, chaotic collage layouts, and anything that feels like a utility tool rather than a polished interior design experience.

## Design Principles

- Make the experience feel effortless and emotionally rewarding.
- Lead with visual confidence and clarity over feature overload.
- Use premium, calm composition that supports the product story.
- Keep the interface approachable for first-time visitors while still feeling polished.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support WCAG 2.1 AA-level contrast and readable typography, with strong focus states and touch-friendly controls. Reduced motion should be respected, and the experience should remain clear for users with low vision or color sensitivity.
